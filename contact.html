What to write to us about

  • Corrections. If something in a guide is wrong or out of date, tell us which article and what needs fixing. A link to a source helps.
  • Questions and suggestions. Ideas for product categories or buying questions you'd like us to cover.
  • Privacy requests. Questions about the information described in our privacy policy.

A note for brands and PR

We don't sell sponsored posts, guaranteed coverage or link placements, and we won't reply to requests for them. You're welcome to send product information or specifications. Whether and how we cover a product is entirely an editorial decision; see our editorial approach.

What we can't help with

We don't sell products, so we can't help with orders, returns, shipping or warranty claims. Please contact the retailer or manufacturer directly.
