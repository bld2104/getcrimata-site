Independent buying guides for home goods and consumer electronics

Getcrimata is a product research site. We explain how everyday products differ, which specifications matter and which are mostly marketing, so you can make a decision that fits your home and budget.

We don't publish star ratings, sponsored placements or "deal of the day" roundups. Each guide lays out the trade-offs and the questions to ask before you buy. You can read how we work on the About page, and how the site is funded in our affiliate disclosure.
